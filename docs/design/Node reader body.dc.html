Check yourself
01

What is the first question to ask about any table?

What does one row represent. Until you can answer it, no aggregate you compute means anything.
02

Name three ways a null can appear and why they differ.

Missing (we never captured it), unknown (we captured that we do not know), not-applicable (the question does not apply). Treating all three as zero silently changes every average.
03

How would you spot a number stored as text?

It left-aligns, aggregates skip it, and its type reads as object or varchar rather than numeric.
The mistake almost everyone makes

Opening a dataset and going straight to a chart. You get an answer in four minutes and spend two days finding out the grain was wrong.
