Who we are

Jintu is a free, self-paced roadmap platform for learning from curated free content. We are the data fiduciary for the information described here. To reach us about anything on this page, write to privacy@jintu.in.

You must be 18 or older

Jintu is not open to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information about children. Our product tracks your progress, streaks and points over time, which is a form of profiling, and we are not permitted to do that to a minor. If you tell us you are under 18, we will not sign you up.
