How glossary terms work
  • Each roadmap has its own glossary file, written and reviewed like its lessons. No live AI definitions
  • One line per meaning, in plain words, 25 words at most
  • Only the first use of a term in a lesson gets the dotted underline
  • Every term links back to the lesson that teaches it. A term used before it is taught fails the Order lens
  • Phone: a bottom sheet. Desktop: a popover beside the word. Both close on Esc, tap outside, or the term again
