Pricing

Free. All of it.

Every roadmap, every lesson, every curated link. No subscription, no trial that expires, no card asked for. There is nothing on this site you can pay for today.

Open a roadmap You do not need an account to read one.

What free includes

  • Every roadmap, in full, readable without an account
  • Progress, streaks and points across every roadmap you start
  • Notes and highlights, private to you, exportable any time
  • Every link opened and checked by a person before it shipped
  • No adverts, and no selling of anything about you

What free does not mean

  • A job, an interview, or an introduction to any employer
  • One-to-one mentoring. That arrives only if enough people ask
  • Anything that expires: the roadmaps stay free and public

How this stays free

Nothing here is priced today, and we are not going to pretend we have settled how it will be paid for. What we will commit to is the order of events: the roadmaps, your progress and your profile stay free, and if that ever changes this page changes first.
