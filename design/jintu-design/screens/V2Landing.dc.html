How this stays free

Questions people ask first

Is this only for people who code?

No. The job guides cover ten fields, from healthcare and finance to design and marketing. Roadmaps open one at a time, and the first one is for software.

Do I need an account?

No. Every lesson is readable without one. An account keeps where you stopped, your streak and your points.

How long is a lesson?

About 15 minutes. Each one is a few small steps you do yourself.

Will this get me a job?

We do not promise that. Jintu teaches the skills, one step at a time. It does not introduce you to employers.

My field is not here. What now?

Tell us what you want to learn. Requests decide which roadmap gets written next.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is nothing on the site you can pay for today. If that ever changes, the pricing page changes first.

Your first lesson takes about 15 minutes.
